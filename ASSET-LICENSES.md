# Artwork attribution

The number artwork and optional dust textures are selected from the existing
TQNL Redstone Numbers resource-pack project. That project's asset provenance
and licence qualifications continue to apply; the MIT code licence does not
relicense third-party artwork.

- Vanilla Tweaks: https://vanillatweaks.net/ and https://vanillatweaks.net/terms/
- Redstone Tweaks by RexxStone: https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/redstone-tweaks/
  Adapted material is retained under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes for this mod: sampled number regions are cropped, nearest-neighbour
scaled into uniform cells, and arranged as a private bitmap font. No original
visible glyph pixels are redesigned. The optional cosmetic pack contains only
the three selected dust textures, without number models or shaders.

The mod is a free community modification, not an official Minecraft product.
It is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

The clean-line textures are also included as a disabled-by-default built-in
resource pack. They are byte-identical to the previously selected artwork.
The TQNL branding icon is reused unchanged from the previous shader/resource
pack editions, for the mod and both cosmetic pack distributions.
